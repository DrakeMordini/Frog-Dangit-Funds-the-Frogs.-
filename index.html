Why we're doing this

Small studios. Big ideas.

Frog Dangit LLC wants to partner with the Triangle Interactive Arts Collective (TIAC) and make a sizeable contribution toward hosting more events for local indie game developers. We want more opportunities to meet, showcase games, share ideas, and build a stronger creative community across the RTP area.

Our fundraising target is $3,000–$5,000. We will share updates about the campaign and how funds are allocated as plans are finalized.

More developer events

Support opportunities for local creators to get together and show what they're making.

More indie connections

Help strengthen the network of developers and players in the Triangle.
