# Frog Dangit Funds the Frogs!

Frog Dangit LLC wants to partner with the **Triangle Interactive Arts Collective (TIAC)** to make a sizeable donation toward supporting our local indie game development community!

Our goal is to raise **$3,000–$5,000** to help TIAC host more events, bring developers together, and create more opportunities for indie creators to showcase their work.

We're passionate about growing the local indie scene, connecting developers, and helping our community thrive.

**Together, we're going to take over the RTP area, one indie game at a time!**
